# Delete your Flap Fit online profile and data

Status: public-page draft. This page must be published at the exact HTTPS URL supplied to Google
Play, App Store Connect, and `EXPO_PUBLIC_ACCOUNT_DELETION_URL`.

Flap Fit does not require email, phone, or a password. An anonymous online profile is created only if
you accept the Competition Terms and use Compete.

## Delete in the app

1. Open Flap Fit.
2. Open the Info tab.
3. Select Delete online profile.
4. Confirm Delete.

This permanently deletes the anonymous Supabase authentication user and associated player name,
leaderboard scores, challenges, challenge attempts, and blocks. A minimized moderation report may
remain until review and for up to 180 days after resolution for safety, abuse prevention, and legal
obligations, but the deleted account's user identifier is removed from it. Local bird, level, score,
and training history stored only on the phone are not deleted by this action.

## Request deletion without the app

Email support@flapfitapp.com with the subject "Flap Fit account deletion." Include your
player name and any challenge code, exercise leaderboard, approximate score, and approximate date
that can help us locate the anonymous profile. Do not send camera images, workout videos, passwords,
government identification, or other sensitive information.

Because the profile has no email or password, we may need additional non-sensitive competition

cannot safely identify the profile, we will explain the next available step.
