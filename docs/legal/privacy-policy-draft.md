## Information kept on your device

Flap Fit stores your selected bird, game progress, scores, tracked training time, exercise history,
and settings on your device. This information is not sent to Flap Fit unless a feature described
below says otherwise. You can remove local information by clearing the app's data or deleting the
app.

## Camera and Record & Play

Flap Fit uses the front camera during gameplay to estimate body landmarks and translate movement
into bird movement. Pose estimation runs on your device using a model bundled with the app. During
normal Play, Flap Fit does not record or upload camera images.

If you select Record & Play, Flap Fit can request microphone permission and create a video containing
the camera view, game graphics, your voice, and workout sounds. The video remains in temporary app
storage only so you can use your device's share sheet. Flap Fit does not upload or receive the video.
A destination you choose in the share sheet processes the video under its own privacy terms. The
temporary file is removed when you leave the result screen or begin another run, subject to normal
operating-system cache behavior.

## Optional online competition

You can play Flap Fit without using Compete. If you accept the Competition Terms and use Compete,
Flap Fit creates an anonymous Supabase authentication identifier and processes:

- your chosen player name;
- the exercise category and score submitted to a leaderboard;
- challenge codes, targets, and attempts;
- reports and blocks you submit; and
- ordinary service, security, and error logs generated when the service handles a request.

We use this information to operate leaderboards and challenges, keep your anonymous competition
profile working, enforce community rules, prevent abuse, investigate reports, secure the service,
and comply with law. Player names and competition results can be public to other players. The app
does not send camera images, Record & Play videos, or your complete local training history to the
competition service.

## Retention

Local information remains until you clear app data or delete the app. Online profiles and current
leaderboard scores remain until you delete the online profile. Challenges expire after 30 days and
are removed with their attempts by the daily production cleanup process. Pending reports remain until
reviewed. Resolved moderation reports are removed 180 days after resolution. If either party deletes
their account before then, the report is retained without that account's user identifier. Service
logs and backups follow the production provider configuration.

## Your choices and deletion

You may decline camera permission, use normal Play instead of Record & Play, avoid Compete, or stop
using Compete at any time. In the app, open Info and select Delete online profile to permanently
delete the anonymous competition account and its associated profile, scores, challenges, attempts,
and blocks. A minimized moderation report may remain until review and for up to 180 days after
resolution, but the deleted account's user identifier is removed from it. Local game progress stays
on the device.

If you no longer have the app, visit https://flapfitapp.com/delete-account/ and follow the request
instructions. Privacy questions and applicable access, correction, objection, restriction, or
deletion requests may be sent to support@flapfitapp.com. We may need enough information
to locate the anonymous competition profile without exposing or deleting another player's data.

## Children

Flap Fit is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect online competition
information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child provided information contrary
to this policy.

## Safety and changes

Flap Fit is a fitness game, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any
condition. We may update this policy when the app or law changes. Material changes will be posted at
this URL with a new effective date, and renewed consent will be requested when required.
