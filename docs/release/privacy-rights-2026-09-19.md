# Required advertising privacy access — September 19, 2026

The owner authorized only necessary advertising privacy handling for the applicable region/circumstances. This patch repairs the existing Info → Ad privacy settings entry. No new question, standalone screen, discretionary ad-free switch, reporting button, or competition button was added. The previously proposed custom interface remains unapproved.

Info now reads the native Google privacy-entry requirement independently of advertising eligibility or SDK initialization. When Google returns REQUIRED, the existing entry can open its form even when neither ads nor RevenueCat is permitted. NOT_REQUIRED hides the entry. Unknown/error preserves an already-known requirement and never establishes permission. Reading stored status is not a replacement for the permitted fresh UMP consent update on launch and does not resolve location or age.

The native form shares the existing presentation barrier: an unsettled result ad must dismiss or fail before it opens. A late lookup after game entry, backgrounding or leaving Info cannot present a form. Repeated actions share one request; returning to the lobby cannot start a second form. A Google response permitting ads still needs the independent current age/processing decision; RevenueCat remains separately gated. Ad placement/cadence and the accepted game/calibration/recording/sharing sources are unchanged.

The underlying reporting-withdrawal action applies its refusal synchronously, then waits for durable saving and the native transport-stop acknowledgement separately. Rejection or a 10-second wait limit reports unconfirmed status, never successful withdrawal; the refusal remains in force. An explicit retry retries an uncertain native stop without re-enabling reporting. A newer explicit grant cannot be reported as stopped. A separate participation action preserves authenticated deletion/support and does not load monetization. These service methods add no user-facing controls by themselves.
