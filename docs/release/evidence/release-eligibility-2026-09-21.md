## NY and regional basis — no invented blanket exemption

NY's [covered-user definition](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/899-EE) requires the location/known-minor or primarily-minor-service conditions; a general-audience workout game with a 13+ minimum is not thereby established to be primarily directed to minors. The [territorial section](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/899-LL) separately addresses in-state activity and the wholly-outside exception. The [Attorney General guidance](https://ag.ny.gov/child-data-protection-act-guidance) distinguishes actual age knowledge, covered processing and necessity. A current No or foreign country is **not** stored as a wholly-outside provider/data-history assessment. The correction removes the unsupported inference that lack of that assessment proves every foreign teen covered; it preserves known positive applicability/history. Existing providers' physical processing locations and legacy unknown user travel history are not independently certified. No provider-zero-data or universal statutory exemption is claimed.

For actual known NY minor context, current ad/reporting processing is not asserted strictly necessary and is stopped. The commercial audience restriction applies independent of consent and does not withhold service **because** a purpose was refused ([899-ff](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/899-FF)). CT minor necessity remains unestablished for the configured multi-buyer route ([42-529a](https://prdext2.cga.ct.gov/2026/sup/chap_743jj.htm)); those minors are outside this release. QC uses the existing reviewed identification/location-related route ([private-sector law](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/P-39.1)); exclusion avoids adding an optional activation route to this release. These are application/release decisions, not claims Apple mandates these precise questions or guarantees acceptance. NPA/RDP and Google privacy choices remain, not a zero-personal-data claim. AU/MX authority thresholds retain their previously documented basis; no new regional research or exemption was invented.
