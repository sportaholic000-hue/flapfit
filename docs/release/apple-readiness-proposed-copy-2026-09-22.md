# Historical review-copy proposal

The owner subsequently authorized corrections. See [the applied corrections and current verification](apple-review-corrections-2026-09-22.md). The original proposal below is retained as historical context, not current implementation status.

These are copy-only proposals from the September 22 readiness audit. Nothing below changes eligibility, advertising decisions, the website, Apple settings, or the app. No list of advertising-exempt ages or regions is proposed.

## Apple reviewer notes: replace the setup explanation in the Ads paragraph

After an initial completed run, Flap Fit requires any unanswered age/location setup and the displayed notice to be completed before another run. Setup is available after a selected result exit and through Info > Privacy & choices. If a user closes unfinished setup, the next game entry offers Finish setup. Closing a sheet does not save an answer or acknowledge the notice. Saved answers are retained on the device. Reporting remains a separate choice; stopping RevenueCat reporting does not disable independently permitted Google ads. Google UMP presents its form when required, and Info > Ad privacy settings reopens its choices when available. Lack of ad inventory does not prevent gameplay.

Retain the existing factual ad cadence, recording, camera, competition, deletion, safety, and support instructions. Explain Apple's age sheet separately from the app's own questions. Replace the last paragraph's blanket description of older systems with:

Where Apple's age-range service is unavailable, the app offers a local age-band question when applicable. That answer is not Apple-verified and cannot satisfy a mandatory Apple authorization check. The app's 13+ minimum and any required Apple access restrictions still apply. Compete has separate eligibility and participation checks; accepting competition rules does not complete advertising setup.

## Live privacy policy: setup wording

Replace the conflicting setup sentences in “Service providers and transfers” and “Age, region, and Apple authorization” with one consistent account:

Flap Fit presents setup after an initial completed run. Before another run, complete any unanswered age or location questions and acknowledge the displayed notice. You can reopen setup through Info > Privacy & choices. Closing setup leaves unanswered questions and an unread notice incomplete. Your answers are saved on this device. Flap Fit's 13+ minimum and any required Apple authorization checks apply. Regional advertising restrictions do not exclude eligible players from gameplay. Ad availability, reporting preferences, and online competition participation are separate from completing setup.

Retain the accurate statement that camera access is required for all gameplay and microphone access is optional. Preserve the existing explanation of required Google forms and provider-specific choices; do not describe notice acknowledgement as permission for every processing purpose.

## Live terms: replace the outdated setup assertion

Flap Fit is intended for ages 13 and older in Australia, Mexico, Canada and the United States. Regional advertising restrictions affect advertising and ad performance reporting, not gameplay eligibility. After an initial completed run, finish any unanswered setup questions and acknowledge the displayed notice before starting another run. Required Apple authorization, the minimum-age rule and separate online competition rules still apply. See the Privacy Policy for details.

## Privacy and deletion pages: distinguish legacy run records

New leaderboard results use a single score submission and do not create a server run-verification record. Legacy run records from earlier versions or testing expire six hours after creation and are removed by the daily cleanup after being expired for seven days. Deleting an online profile also removes associated legacy run records.

Retain the existing current profile/score, moderation, authorization-record and provider-retention disclosures. Do not promise deletion of independent provider records through competition-profile deletion.

## In-app status copy: narrow “Gameplay is available”

The eligibility status currently checks the audience/Apple rules but does not check unfinished setup. On the privacy page, use “Age eligibility confirmed” only when age eligibility is actually confirmed; otherwise use a factual “Review your setup below.” For the older-system explanation, use:

This system cannot provide the age check used by Flap Fit. Choose your age range below to finish setup. Your answer stays on this device, and your saved progress is retained.

Final wording should be conditionally tied to the existing verified state. This proposal changes display text only, not what enables a run or an advertisement.

## Separate unresolved functional issue

Copy edits cannot complete a new applicable Google data-processing withdrawal. “Contact us” currently opens an email draft; there is no implemented support-to-installation completion mechanism for that request when Google's privacy form is unavailable. Previously saved refusals and the separate reporting stop already work. A scoped implementation decision is still needed; this document does not authorize or propose a general ads-off button.
