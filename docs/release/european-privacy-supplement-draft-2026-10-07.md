## Prepared wording for the launch-scope paragraph

Flap Fit is intended for people aged 13 or older in Canada, the United States, Australia, Mexico, the 27 European Union countries, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Availability depends on the applicable App Store storefront. The game and optional online services have different requirements. Eligibility to play does not automatically authorize advertising, optional reporting or leaderboard participation.

## Prepared wording for country and age handling

We use the current network country to apply the relevant setup and privacy rules. This is not a declaration of your home province, state or country. Locally saved province/state answers remain separate. The country lookup may receive an IP address as part of its response, but the app discards that response and retains only the limited country state needed for the current session. We do not forward that lookup response to advertising or reporting providers.

Where available, Apple's age service provides a range used to determine eligibility and applicable safeguards. The app does not store the raw Apple age range or transmit it to advertising or reporting providers. Where Apple's service is unsupported, the existing local age-range question is used. A declined or failed supported Apple check is not replaced with a self-declared answer. Age 13 is the minimum game audience, not a universal age for consenting to optional data processing.

## Prepared wording for Google advertising

In the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message presents the applicable advertising choices. Flap Fit requests restricted, non-personalized advertising with a PG maximum rating. Depending on your choices and Google's eligibility decision, non-personalized or limited ads may be available, or no ad may be served. Non-personalized ads can still require consent for device storage and associated processing. Google's privacy-options form is available through Manage Privacy Choices when Google requires it. Choosing not to permit a particular use must not be interpreted as permission for that use.

Google may use limited device storage for invalid-traffic protection when serving programmatic limited ads. The publisher's final notice must state the assessed applicable basis for that processing; Google's product setting alone is not a legal assessment.

## Prepared wording for independent RevenueCat reporting

RevenueCat ad-performance reporting is separate from Google's advertising choices. In supported European countries, it starts only after you explicitly allow it in Ad performance reporting. RevenueCat receives a separate random app identifier, app/device information and ad loads, views, clicks and revenue to measure ad performance. It does not receive your camera footage, recordings, exercises, scores or training history. You can decline and keep playing, or stop reporting later in Info. Acknowledging the general setup notice or making a choice in Google's message does not authorize RevenueCat reporting. Withdrawing permission stops future optional reporting; it does not retroactively change the lawfulness of prior consent-based processing.

## Prepared wording for competition

Leaderboards are optional. Choosing to participate invokes the separate competition terms and notice. The existing service uses an anonymous profile/session identifier, your selected public name, exercise category, score and necessary service/security data to operate the requested leaderboard. Names and scores are public to other participants. Camera footage, recordings and local training history are not submitted. Report, Block and Delete controls remain available. Advertising consent does not authorize leaderboard participation, and leaderboard participation does not authorize ad-performance reporting.

## Prepared wording for European rights

Depending on the applicable law and processing basis, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, receive eligible data in a portable format, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing. You may complain to the relevant data-protection authority. Contact us using the existing privacy contact or the applicable representative contact published here. Not every right applies to every processing operation, and we may need proportionate information to identify the relevant record.
