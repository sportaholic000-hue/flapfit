## Deadline and eligibility

- Devpost closes September 30, 2026 at 11:45 PM Pacific. In Halifax this is October 1 at 3:45 AM
  Atlantic Daylight Time.
- A normal-category entry needs a fully published public App Store, Google Play, or Samsung Galaxy
  Store listing by the deadline. TestFlight, Play internal testing, or an App Store submission still
  waiting for review does not count.
- The first public store release must occur during the July 31 to September 30, 2026 submission
  window.
- The published app must be downloadable in the United States.
- Flap Fit's qualifying integration is RevenueCat Ads. Version 1.0 has no purchase, subscription,
  paywall, or premium feature.
- A public/open-source repository is not required for the normal categories. Keep the Flap Fit
  source repository private unless a selected category explicitly requires otherwise.

## Flap Fit evidence to preserve

- Exact released commit hash and App Store version/build number.
- Screenshot of the live store listing and its public URL.
- Screenshot or export proving the RevenueCat Ads project receives real test/production ad events
  from the released integration without fitness, camera, player, or score data.
- Final two-minute video source plus the public YouTube/Vimeo URL.
- Final icon and selected contest screenshot.
- A short statement that Flap Fit is free and ad-supported with no premium features. If the Devpost
  form asks for a free trial or promo code, explain that there is no locked purchase to unlock and
  request official clarification if the form still requires a value.

## Suggested demo-video sequence

Use `shipaton-demo-script.md` for a 1:54 shot-by-shot capture plan. Keep the finished video under two
minutes and film the exact released native build on a physical device:

1. Open on a real workout controlling the bird, with physical-player and game footage from the
   same run. Follow with the home screen, six exercise birds, and Sky/Live Camera choices.
2. Show two-position calibration followed by continuous camera-controlled gameplay.
3. Live Camera Record & Play and the resulting native share action.
4. Bird evolution and tracked-training Stats.
5. Optional real Compete leaderboard, Daily Challenge and complete gameplay video sharing, if physically verified.
6. A brief, truthful RevenueCat Ads result-action demonstration from the released candidate.

Do not claim that pose tracking proves perfect form, that calories or complete repetitions are
measured, that recordings are uploaded, or that a leaderboard contains live users when demo data is
being shown.
